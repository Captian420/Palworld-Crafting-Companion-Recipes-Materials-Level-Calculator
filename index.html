Machine-readable Palworld.gg snapshot

The app loads the v1.0 recipe snapshot published by Maurixs, whose repository states the item/recipe/tech/icon data was extracted from Palworld.gg. Current patch changes are corrected in-app.

GitHub data snapshot
How availability is calculated: an item with its own Technology Level uses that level. A processed recipe with no individual technology node (for example, food or a refined material) inherits the minimum level of its required crafting station when that station is known. Schematic/drop-only or otherwise non-level-gated recipes are separated behind the “Show schematic / special recipes” option instead of being incorrectly shown at Level 1.

Current-patch corrections: this build applies verified post-1.0 launch changes for Jetragon's Missile Launcher, Aquatic Construction Kit, Crude Oil Extractor, High-Pressure Crude Oil Extractor, and Large Power Generator. The baseline machine-readable dataset is from July 2026; current online references are used for those later recipe/progression changes.

Acquisition & locations: the app now loads resource farming recommendations, item acquisition routes, merchant coordinates, and Pal habitat/alpha-location data from the v1.0 Palworld knowledge datasets. For very large world-node layers, the panel shows best coordinates locally and links to Palworld.gg's complete interactive layer rather than rendering thousands of rows.

Icons: icons are loaded from the published Palworld.gg-derived sprite set. If an image fails to load, the app falls back to text initials instead of breaking the card.
